Bali Temple & Rice Terrace Trail

Bali, Indonesia

Overview

Discover the spiritual heart of Bali on this immersive 5-day guided tour through ancient temples, emerald rice terraces, and hidden waterfalls. Designed for curious travelers, this journey blends cultural encounters with breathtaking natural landscapes, offering an authentic glimpse into Balinese life.

From the iconic sea temple of Tanah Lot to the cascading rice paddies of Tegallalang, each day brings a new adventure. You'll participate in traditional ceremonies, learn the art of Balinese cooking, and trek through some of the island's most stunning scenery — all with the guidance of a passionate local expert.

Highlights

Visit ancient temples including Tanah Lot and Uluwatu
Trek through the famous Tegallalang rice terraces
Traditional Balinese cooking class with a local family
Swim in a hidden jungle waterfall
Attend a traditional Kecak fire dance performance
Explore Ubud's art galleries and artisan workshops

Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival & Welcome Ceremony
Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport where your guide will greet you with a traditional flower garland. Transfer to your boutique hotel in Ubud, nestled among lush rice paddies. After settling in, join a welcome ceremony with offerings and blessings at a nearby village temple, followed by a group dinner featuring authentic Balinese cuisine.
Day 2
Temple Circuit
Start early with a visit to the sacred Tirta Empul water temple for a traditional purification ritual. Continue to the ancient Gunung Kawi rock-cut shrines, one of Bali's oldest and most dramatic monuments. In the evening, head to the clifftop Uluwatu Temple to witness the mesmerizing Kecak fire dance at sunset over the Indian Ocean.
Day 3
Rice Terraces & Waterfalls
Lace up your walking shoes for a guided trek through the iconic Tegallalang rice terraces, learning about the ancient Subak irrigation system along the way. After lunch at a hillside cafe overlooking the valley, hike to the stunning Tegenungan Waterfall for a refreshing swim in its turquoise pool surrounded by tropical jungle.
Day 4
Culture & Cooking
Spend the morning exploring Ubud's vibrant art scene — visit the Neka Art Museum and browse traditional Batik workshops. After a morning coffee at a local plantation, join a hands-on Balinese cooking class with a local family. Prepare dishes like Nasi Goreng, Sate Lilit, and Lawar using fresh market ingredients, then enjoy your creations together.
Day 5
Free Time & Departure
Your final morning is free to explore at your own pace — enjoy a sunrise yoga session, revisit your favorite spot, or pick up souvenirs at the Ubud Art Market. After a farewell brunch, your guide will transfer you to the airport for your onward journey. Selamat jalan — safe travels!
