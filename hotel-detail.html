Overview

About this property

Nestled on the pristine island of Kunfunadhoo in the Baa Atoll UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, Soneva Fushi is the quintessential desert island hideaway. With 63 spacious villas ranging from one to nine bedrooms, each tucked among dense tropical vegetation, the resort offers an unparalleled experience of barefoot luxury.

Guests can explore the house reef teeming with marine life, dine under the stars at the open-air observatory, or unwind at the award-winning Six Senses Spa. From the castaway cinema to the chocolate room, every detail is designed to create extraordinary memories while maintaining a deep commitment to sustainability.
